Questions before you reach out?

A few answers that might save you a step.

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes — 30 minutes, no cost, no obligation. We'll ask about your business and current bookkeeping setup, then tell you honestly whether we're a good fit.

Do I need to have my books in order first?

Not at all. Many clients come to us with months of missing recordings or messy categorizations. Book cleanup and catch-up are part of what we do.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Plans start at $349/month for up to 100 transactions. Pricing scales with volume and the level of advisory you need. Full pricing is on our Pricing page.

What software do you work in?

QuickBooks and Xero primarily — we're certified in both. If you're not on either yet, we'll help you choose and set up the right one.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Documents are shared through encrypted channels, portal access is password-protected, and we follow standard data handling practices. We never share your data with third parties.
